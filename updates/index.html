Candy Merge 2048 update notes

Everything that changed in the game, newest first · Play Candy Merge 2048 · Strategy guide

Most of the work on Candy Merge after launch went into how the candies feel — how they fall, land, roll and bump — because in a merge game the physics is the game. This log keeps track of each change and the reasoning behind it.

Slower falls

OCTOBER 22, 2026

  • Gravity turned down so candies fall at about 80% of the previous speed (impact forces scaled to match). The old pace made the pile look jittery on tall stacks and gave you less time to read where a candy would land.

Rolling that behaves like rolling

OCTOBER 21, 2026

  • Candies now have real rolling contacts: a candy that lands on a slope spins and rolls instead of sliding, and rolling on the floor slows down naturally.
  • A released candy keeps a little of the motion of your drag and a small random spin, so landings on the floor roll out instead of stopping dead.

World ranking and natural landings

OCTOBER 20, 2026

  • World ranking added: enter a nickname and your best score joins a top-50 list, with your own rank shown underneath. The ranking stores only the nickname and the score.
  • Landings reworked: a real impact bounce, lighter friction and a quick squash on contact. The merge "pop" that nudges neighbours stays; the hop and scale overshoot added the day before were removed because they made tall piles unstable.

Keyboard controls, ads and a tighter gem economy

OCTOBER 19, 2026

  • Keyboard play on desktop: arrow keys or A/D to aim, Space, Enter or Down to drop.
  • Optional rewarded ads: continue after a game over, or collect 20 gems up to five times a day. A short full-screen ad may appear after a game that lasted at least 20 seconds. The game is fully playable with ads blocked.
  • Gem economy tightened — fewer gems from the end-of-game bonus so Shake, Remove and Continue are decisions rather than reflexes.
  • Softer physics overall: capped rebound speeds, merged candies grow into their new size instead of popping to it, so neighbours are nudged rather than launched.
  • Layout fixes for Korean and Japanese text (webfonts, word breaking, title wrapping) and a pass of UI alignment polish.

Candy Merge 2048 1.0

OCTOBER 19, 2026 · FIRST RELEASE

  • Eleven fruit candies from Blueberry to Watermelon, drawn as glossy sprites.
  • Jar physics, NEXT preview, auto-drop mode, Shake and Remove items, Continue with a clearing circle.
  • Gems from play, discovery, daily bonus and a 30-minute treasure chest; a candy collection screen; recent-games and weekly-best records kept in your browser.
  • Korean, English and Japanese, detected automatically and switchable in the menu.
